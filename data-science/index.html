Identity

I am a PhD candidate in Information Science with a Data Science concentration. My research uses machine learning, natural language processing, contextual embeddings, large language models, and information retrieval to analyze corporate disclosures and other complex information environments.

Dissertation

Dissertation research

Three studies from my dissertation, Computational Analysis of Corporate Disclosures: Measuring Change, Identifying Novel Information, and Retrieving Evidence from MD&A.

Contextual MD&A Change

Problem
Develops a semantic measure of year-over-year disclosure change using section alignment, contextual embeddings, chunk matching, and document-level aggregation.
Dataset
Year-over-year MD&A sections of U.S. corporate filings
