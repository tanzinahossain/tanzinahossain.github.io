Teaching

Teaching philosophy

My teaching focuses on a practical question: what should students be able to do by the end of the class? I begin with what students already know, explain new concepts in clear language, and give them opportunities to apply those concepts and defend their reasoning. In finance and quantitative courses, I connect theory with real financial data, business problems, and managerial decisions.
