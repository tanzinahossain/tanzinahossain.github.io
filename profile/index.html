Profile

Professional biography

I am a PhD candidate in Information Science with a Data Science concentration at the University of North Texas and an Associate Professor of Finance at Daffodil International University, currently on study leave. My research combines finance and accounting questions with econometrics, machine learning, natural language processing, and information retrieval. I bring more than sixteen years of university teaching experience in corporate finance, investments, financial markets, and research methods.
